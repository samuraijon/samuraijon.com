Across the medical device lifecycle

From designing microscale transdermal platforms and instrumented implants to signal processing, health data modelling and auditing manufacturers against regulatory requirements: I've worked on medical devices from the lab bench to the auditor's table.

Publications on ORCID and Google Scholar.

Medical device auditing and regulatory compliance

As a medical device auditor and EMEA client manager at BSI I assessed manufacturers of Class I–III devices (active, passive, implantable and software) against ISO 13485, EU MDR, MDD/UKCA and MDSAP. I reviewed design and development controls, risk management, supplier management, verification and validation, clinical evaluation, post-market surveillance, complaints and CAPA, wrote audit reports and managed client relationships.

Vital signs patch and health data modelling

As a health data scientist at TNO I analysed individual and population health data: heart rate, HRV and respiratory rate from a medical-grade wearable tested on Dutch military pilots and validated against gold-standard devices, plus COVID-19 and obesity trends. I worked with the Dutch Ministries of Health and Defence, used R, Python and Matlab, and secured funding for a data platform for medical device clinical studies.

Instrumented hip implant

At TU Delft I led the development of instrumented hip implants for intraoperative stability and balancing, using optical, electromagnetic and inertial sensors driven by Arduino. Supported by Johnson & Johnson, with preclinical trials at SINTEF (Norway) and CCMIJU (Spain), priority patent filings and government funding to launch a startup. I also supervised Bachelor and Master students and taught in the Medical Technology Design course.

Microneedle drug delivery and biomarker sampling

My PhD at the University of Queensland developed biomechanical and diffusion kinetic models of skin that scale allometrically across species, so microscale devices such as the Nanopatch can move from animal studies to clinical use faster. It included 3D multiphoton microscopy of vaccine diffusion, a clinical study in Linköping, Sweden, and a presentation at the European Society of Biomechanics in Lyon. Thesis DOI 10.14264/uql.2018.594.
