Dr Jonathan CJ Wei

PhD, Biomedical Engineering
BE, Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (IIA Hons)
orcid.org/0000-0003-0222-5425

I'm a biomedical engineer with a global upbringing, having lived in Taiwan, South Africa and Australia. I'm now based in the Netherlands. Probably because of this, everywhere I go, I look and sound like a foreigner.

In 2018, I completed my PhD in Biomedical Engineering at The University of Queensland, where I focused on the key biomechanics of the skin for translating microscale medical devices. I investigated the allometric relationship between animal models and humans to speed up the clinical adaptation of transdermal drug delivery and biomarker sampling platforms such as the Nanopatch. Before that, in 2012, I graduated from UQ in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering with IIA Honours.

Shortly after my PhD, I moved to the Netherlands to join TU Delft as a postdoc, where I developed a force- and motion-sensing hip arthroplasty implant. I supervised Bachelor and Master thesis students and assisted in the Medical Technology Design course. The work led to publications, funded grants and a priority filing of our implant design.

In 2022, I joined TNO as a health data scientist, working on the Health Patch medical-grade wearable that measures heart rate and respiratory rate, and on population-level data including COVID-19 and obesity. Between 2025 and 2026, I qualified as a medical device auditor at BSI, covering ISO 13485, EU MDR, MDD, UKCA and MDSAP for clients from small companies to large multinationals. In 2026, I founded my own ventures.

I code in Matlab, HTML, Arduino, Python and R, and I've used Adobe Creative Cloud since school: Photoshop, Illustrator, Dreamweaver, After Effects, Audition and Lightroom. I do extensive 3D CAD modelling in Siemens Solid Edge.

Outside work, I ride and race my bike. I'm a member of WTOS Delft and RWC Ahoy, and with UQCC I progressed from university cycling to racing A grade in local crits. I'm active in content creation, graphic design and special effects, play the guitar and piano, and invest in a portfolio of US and European stocks and ETFs.
